# RUS Electrical — Current Brand / Digital Opportunity Audit

## What already works
- The **red lightning bolt + black + yellow** palette instantly communicates electrical work.
- The phrase **“done in a proper way”** gives the brand personality and should be retained or refined, not discarded.
- The existing business has visible customer reviews and a public service history.
- The business appears to cover multiple useful service categories, giving enough content for a strong SEO website.

## Main weaknesses
### 1. Current identity is a flyer, not a scalable brand system
The supplied visual combines logo, photos, contact details, a large bolt and multiple typography styles into one graphic. It does not provide a clean standalone logo for a website header, favicon, vehicle branding, workwear, social media or quotation documents.

### 2. Low-resolution / dated imagery
The existing job photos are useful proof, but the image quality and framing are too weak for a premium Sandton-facing website.

### 3. No coherent digital hierarchy
The current brand does not clearly separate residential, commercial/industrial, emergency, compliance and backup-power services.

### 4. Weak premium positioning
RUS Electrical appears to have a stronger reputation than its visual presentation suggests. The new website should make the business feel established, safe, professional and technically capable.

### 5. Contact and location information needs consolidation
Public sources disagree on the current address and hours. The website must use one verified data source after owner confirmation.

## New brand direction
Keep the heritage:
- black
- red
- yellow
- lightning-bolt motif

Upgrade it into a cleaner technical identity:
- deep graphite / black base
- safety yellow
- controlled red accents
- warm white
- industrial grey

The regenerated logo in this pack uses an **R + lightning-bolt monogram** and keeps the recognisable colour DNA while making it suitable for web, workwear and vehicle applications.

## Recommended positioning
**RUS Electrical**
Reliable electrical work for homes, businesses and properties across Sandton and Johannesburg North.

Suggested headline language:
- **Power. Safety. Done Properly.**
- **Electrical work done the proper way.**
- **Reliable electrical solutions, from call-out to compliance.**

Do not claim certifications, guarantees, response times or 24/7 availability unless the owner confirms them.
