## OpenCode implementation guidance
Use the transparent logo in the navbar. Use the premium logo PNG for social-card/mockup contexts. Prefer WebP versions for production photography. Use `next/image` and proper responsive `sizes`.

The generated workwear and van graphics are **proposal imagery**, not evidence that the business currently owns these branded uniforms/vehicles. Do not present them as documentary photographs on a live site without client approval.
